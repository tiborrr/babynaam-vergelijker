How Pairwise Baby Name Ranking Works

Choosing a baby name together shouldn't be an argument. The Baby Name Ranker is a pairwise consensus app designed for couples. Instead of staring at an overwhelming list of 500 names, this tool asks you a simple question repeatedly: "Do you prefer Name A or Name B?". Using a mathematically efficient merge-sort algorithm, it builds a complete top-to-bottom ranking for both partners. When you're both finished, compare your lists to instantly reveal your mutual favorites.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do couples choose a baby name without arguing? By using a pairwise comparison tool, each partner evaluates names head-to-head in a private ranking session. Once both are finished, the app calculates the mathematical consensus, revealing the mutual top choices without the stress of debating every single name.

Is our baby name data private? Yes, completely private. The application runs 100% locally in your web browser. We do not use tracking cookies, and no data is ever uploaded to a server. Your rankings stay on your device unless you manually export them.

Can we use lists from different cultures? Yes! The app includes curated starter packs for Dutch, Arabic, French, Spanish, Nordic, and English baby names. You can mix and match these lists or paste your own custom list of names.
